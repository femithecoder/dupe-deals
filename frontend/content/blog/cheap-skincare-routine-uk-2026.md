## Frequently asked

**Do I actually need to double cleanse?** Only if you wear sunscreen or makeup, which most people should be wearing at least one of. If you wear neither, one gentle cleanse is enough and the oil step is a product you do not need.

**Is Korean skincare better, or just cheaper?** Mostly it is better value rather than categorically better. Formulation standards are high, competition is fierce and packaging sizes are generous. The actives are frequently the same ones in far more expensive Western products.

**What is missing from this routine?** Sunscreen, and it is the most important omission. Nothing above matters as much as daily SPF, and none of our feeds carry a facial sunscreen worth recommending, so we are not pretending otherwise.

**Does a toner do anything?** A hydrating one does: it puts water on the skin for the moisturiser to hold. The astringent alcohol-based toners that gave the category its reputation are a different product and largely gone from good ranges.

**Can I skip straight to the moisturiser?** You can, and plenty of people with unbothered skin do fine. The steps above are in order of how much difference they make, which is why cleansing is first and the mist is optional.
