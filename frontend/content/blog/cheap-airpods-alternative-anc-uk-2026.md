## What "cheap AirPods alternative" usually gets wrong

Most searches for this land on generic no-name earbuds from marketplace listings with no real brand, no software support, and specs that are difficult to verify independently. That's a real risk with true wireless earbuds specifically, since fit, seal quality, and noise cancellation are hard to judge from a listing alone. A name you can actually look up matters here more than in most product categories.

Nothing is a real, independently reviewed brand, not a marketplace mystery listing, known specifically for a transparent, minimalist design aesthetic that draws comparisons to Apple's own products. At £67.14, it undercuts even the best current UK deal on the base AirPods 4, and includes active noise cancellation the base AirPods 4 lacks entirely.

**Noise cancellation:** Rated up to 45dB active noise cancellation, a genuine ANC implementation rather than basic passive isolation, putting it in the same conversation as AirPods 4's separate £179 ANC variant, at well under half the price.

**Audio quality:** 11mm driver with dual vents, Hi-Res Audio certified up to 24-bit/96kHz over LDAC (on Android devices that support the codec; iPhone users will get AAC instead, which is still solid but not the full Hi-Res spec).

**Battery life:** Genuinely varies by settings, 6 to 9.5 hours per charge depending on whether ANC and the higher-bitrate LDAC codec are switched on, with the case extending total playtime to around 42.5 hours. Worth setting expectations here: leaving ANC on will meaningfully cut into that per-charge number, which is true of most ANC earbuds, not a flaw specific to this pair.

**Durability:** IP54-rated earbuds, meaning real dust and splash resistance for gym use or being caught in light rain, though the charging case itself is only IPX2, lightly splash-resistant rather than properly water-resistant, so don't submerge the case.

**Multitasking:** Multipoint connection lets you pair to two devices simultaneously, useful for switching between a laptop and phone without manually reconnecting each time, a feature the base AirPods don't offer.

**Verdict:** Cheaper than even AirPods 4's best current sale price, with ANC included as standard rather than a separate, pricier tier.

## What you'd actually be giving up

Apple's ecosystem integration, specifically the instant pairing, Find My tracking, and seamless device-switching within Apple's own ecosystem, isn't something a third-party earbud fully replicates. If that ecosystem lock-in is the actual reason you want AirPods specifically, no alternative, however good, fully replaces it. If what you actually want is good ANC wireless earbuds at a fair price, that's a different, more answerable question, and it's the one this comparison is addressing.

## Frequently asked

**Do these work properly with an iPhone?** Yes, over standard Bluetooth and AAC, though the LDAC Hi-Res Audio codec specifically is an Android feature, iPhone users won't get that particular benefit but will get full ANC, calls, and controls.

**Is 45dB ANC actually noticeable, or just a marketing number?** Genuine active noise cancellation at this level should meaningfully reduce consistent background noise (traffic, aircon, office chatter), though no ANC earbuds fully eliminate sudden sharp sounds like a door slam. It's a real, usable implementation, not a token gesture.

**Is IP54 good enough for the gym?** For sweat and light rain, yes. It's not a swimming-rated earbud, if you specifically need waterproofing for swimming, that's a different product category with a higher IP rating requirement.
