Apple's AirPods 4 carry a £159 RRP, but they [regularly sell for around £88 to £89](https://www.techradar.com/news/cheapest-airpods-prices) at the best current UK deals. That's the real number to beat — and worth knowing upfront, the base AirPods 4 don't even come with active noise cancellation. You'd need the pricier ANC version (officially £179) for that.

## What "cheap AirPods alternative" usually gets wrong

Most searches for this land on generic, no-name earbuds from marketplace listings, with no real brand and specs that are hard to check. That's a real risk with wireless earbuds specifically — fit, seal, and noise cancelling are hard to judge from a listing alone. A name you can actually look up matters more here than in most product categories.

## Our pick

### [Nothing Ear (a)](/product/39): £67.14 (was £82.99)

![Nothing Ear (a) true wireless earbuds](https://assets.media-quzo.co.uk/site/catalogue/large/2025/08/06/nothing-ear-a-product-type-headset-connectivity-technology-true-wireless-stereo-tws-bluetooth-r-407303.webp)

Nothing is a real, independently reviewed brand, not a mystery marketplace listing — known for a see-through, minimal design that draws obvious comparisons to Apple's own. At £67.14, it beats even the best current UK deal on the base AirPods 4, and comes with active noise cancelling, which the base AirPods 4 don't have at all.

**Noise cancelling:** Rated up to 45dB, a real ANC system, not basic passive isolation. That puts it close to AirPods 4's separate £179 ANC version, at under half the price.

**Sound quality:** An 11mm driver with dual vents, and Hi-Res Audio support up to 24-bit/96kHz over LDAC (on Android phones that support it; iPhone gets AAC instead, still solid, just not the full Hi-Res spec).

**Battery life:** Varies with your settings — 6 to 9.5 hours per charge depending on whether ANC and the higher-quality LDAC codec are switched on, with the case stretching total playtime to around 42.5 hours. Worth knowing: leaving ANC on will noticeably cut into that number, which is true of most ANC earbuds, not just this pair.

**Durability:** The earbuds are IP54-rated, so real dust and splash resistance for the gym or light rain. The charging case is only IPX2 though — lightly splash-resistant, not properly water-resistant, so don't submerge it.

**Multitasking:** You can pair to two devices at once, handy for switching between a laptop and phone without reconnecting each time — something the base AirPods can't do.

## What you'd actually be giving up

Apple's ecosystem — instant pairing, Find My tracking, smooth switching between Apple devices — isn't something any third-party earbuds fully copy. If that's the real reason you want AirPods specifically, no alternative fully replaces it. But if what you actually want is good ANC earbuds at a fair price, that's a different, more answerable question — and it's the one this is about.

## Frequently asked

**Do these work properly with an iPhone?** Yes, over standard Bluetooth and AAC. The LDAC Hi-Res codec specifically is an Android feature — iPhone users still get full ANC, calls, and controls.

**Is 45dB ANC actually noticeable, or just a number on a spec sheet?** Real ANC at this level should noticeably cut steady background noise — traffic, aircon, office chatter — though no ANC earbuds fully block sudden sharp sounds like a door slam. It's a genuine, working feature here.

**Is IP54 good enough for the gym?** Yes, for sweat and light rain. It's not rated for swimming — if you need that, you're looking at a different kind of earbud entirely.
