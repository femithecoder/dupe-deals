Nappies are one of the biggest ongoing costs for new parents. A pack of Pampers Active Fit (Size 4, 44 nappies) costs around £12, which works out to roughly £1,400 a year if your baby goes through 4-5 nappies a day.

Own-brand alternatives cost roughly half that. But do they actually work as well?

## What we tested

We compared four nappies on absorbency, fit, leak protection, and skin sensitivity:

- Pampers Active Fit Size 4 (£12 for 44)
- **Aldi Mamia Ultra Dry Size 4 (£2.99 for 48)**
- Lidl Toujours Size 4 (£3.49 for 46)
- Asda Little Angels Size 4 (£4.50 for 44)

## Absorbency

In independent consumer tests, Aldi Mamia and Lidl Toujours consistently score within 5% of Pampers on absorbency. The difference in overnight performance is negligible for most babies.

## Fit and comfort

Pampers has a slight edge on the stretchy waistband. However, Aldi Mamia introduced an updated fit in 2024 that closes the gap significantly.

## Leak protection

All four performed comparably in standard conditions. Heavy wetters may notice a slight difference with budget brands overnight, and switching up a size often solves this.

## Skin sensitivity

None of the own-brand options contain latex. Pampers uses fragrance in some lines, so parents with sensitive babies actually prefer unscented own-brand alternatives.

## The verdict

**Aldi Mamia is the best Pampers dupe.** At £2.99 for 48 nappies vs £12 for 44, you save over £1,000 a year with no meaningful difference in performance.

If you can't get to Aldi regularly, Asda Little Angels is the next best option and available for home delivery.
