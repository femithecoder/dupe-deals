Search "Fitbit Charge 6 alternative" and most results compare against its £139.99 RRP, which makes almost anything look like a bargain by comparison. The problem is that the Charge 6 [regularly sells for £79 to £90](https://www.trustedreviews.com/deals/googles-fitbit-charge-6-is-now-below-90-saving-you-50-for-prime-day) at the best current UK deals, sometimes dropping under £100 during sales events without needing a special offer code. A fair alternative needs to beat that real, achievable price, not the inflated one printed on the box.

## Why people look for a Fitbit alternative in the first place

Usually it's one of three things: the price, even on sale, still feels steep for a fitness band rather than a full smartwatch; a preference for a different app ecosystem (Samsung Health or Google Fit instead of Fitbit's own app); or simply wanting a second device without paying full Fitbit prices twice for a household. All three of those are solved by price and brand trust, not by a mystery no-name band that happens to be cheap.

## The one that actually beats Fitbit's real price

### [Samsung Galaxy Fit3](/product/33): £76.94 (was £85.99)

![Samsung Galaxy Fit3 fitness tracker](https://assets.media-quzo.co.uk/site/catalogue/large/2024/11/29/samsung-galaxy-fit3-4-06-cm-1-6-amoled-touchscreen-0-256-gb-18-5-g-sm-r390nzsaeub-391973.webp)

A 1.6-inch AMOLED touchscreen band with heart-rate and sleep tracking, from a name-brand manufacturer rather than an unfamiliar budget label. At £76.94, it undercuts even Fitbit's own best current sale price, not just the RRP most "alternative" articles compare against. That's a checkable claim: we priced it against Fitbit's actual current UK deals, not the number on the box.

**Battery life:** Samsung quotes up to 13 days with the always-on display switched off, dropping to roughly 3.5 days if you leave the screen on permanently. That's a genuine advantage over most smartwatches, though closer to the Charge 6's own multi-day battery life than a dramatic leap ahead.

**Water resistance:** Rated 5ATM and IP68, meaning it's built for swimming and everyday water exposure (rain, washing hands, sweat), tested to withstand submersion in up to 1.5 metres of fresh water for 30 minutes. Not rated for diving or high-pressure water like a pressure washer.

**Phone compatibility, honestly:** This is the detail most "alternative" roundups skip. The Galaxy Fit3 is built primarily for Android and pairs fully with Samsung's own ecosystem. It can connect to an iPhone running iOS 12 or later, but Samsung's own documentation is upfront that you won't get the full feature set over iOS, some functionality is Android/Samsung Health-only. If you're an iPhone user who wants full functionality, this is the one honest reason to stick with Fitbit's own app instead, which supports iOS properly.

What you give up versus the Charge 6 on Android: Fitbit's own coaching features and the broader Fitbit/Google Fit ecosystem if you're already invested in it. If you're not already a Fitbit user, that's not much of a loss.

**Verdict:** The rare "alternative" that beats the real price, not just the RRP, as long as you're on Android or don't mind reduced iOS functionality.

## Frequently asked

**Is the Galaxy Fit3 as accurate as a Fitbit for tracking?** Samsung's own sensors are well-regarded for heart-rate and sleep tracking, but Fitbit has a longer track record specifically in fitness coaching features layered on top of the raw data. For basic step, heart-rate, and sleep tracking, both are genuinely capable.

**Does either work with an iPhone?** The Charge 6 does, fully, via Fitbit's own app. The Galaxy Fit3 connects to iPhone but with reduced functionality versus pairing with a Samsung Android phone, so we wouldn't recommend it as an iPhone-first purchase.

**Is a fitness band the same as a smartwatch?** Not quite. Both trackers here are bands built around fitness tracking with basic notifications, not full app-running smartwatches like a Galaxy Watch or Apple Watch. If you want app support and cellular calls, that's a different product category entirely.

## The bottom line

Most "Fitbit Charge 6 alternative" content compares against a price nobody actually pays. Checked against Fitbit's real current UK sale price, the Galaxy Fit3 still comes out cheaper with a name-brand behind it, real water resistance, and genuinely long battery life, as long as you're aware of its reduced iOS functionality going in. That's the actual bar worth clearing, and it's the one we checked.
