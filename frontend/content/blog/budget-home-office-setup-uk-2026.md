Most desk advice is a shopping list in a random order. This is the same six things in the order that helps, which turns out to matter more than which brand you pick.

The headline number, since it is the one people arrive looking for: an [Apple Studio Display is £1,499](https://www.apple.com/uk/shop/buy-mac/studio-display) and Apple does not discount it. A 27-inch 4K panel from Dell is {{price:36}}. That is the widest gap on this page by a distance, and it is also the only one where a straightforward comparison is honest, so we will deal with it properly below rather than pretending the other five have equivalents.

## Fix the height before the pixels

The cheapest meaningful upgrade at a desk is not a screen. It is getting the screen you already own to the right height.

Looking down at a laptop for eight hours is the thing your neck objects to, and no resolution fixes it. A stand or an arm costs under eighty pounds and changes how the day feels; a sharper panel at the wrong height changes nothing except how nice the wrong height looks.

So the order below is deliberate: height, then connection, then the screen itself, then the extras.

## Frequently asked

**Is 4K enough at 27 inches, or do I need 5K?** 4K at 27 inches is about 163 pixels per inch, which is sharp enough that text looks clean and you stop thinking about it. 5K is sharper and you would see it side by side. Whether you would see it a week later, working alone, is a different question and the honest answer for most people is no.

**Do I need a monitor arm if the monitor came with a stand?** Only if the stand puts the screen at the wrong height, which most do, or if you want the desk space back. It is a comfort and space upgrade, not a picture one.

**Will the dock charge my laptop?** This one is USB 3.0 for display and peripherals, not a modern USB-C power delivery dock. If charging over the same cable matters to you, that is a different and more expensive category, and worth knowing before ordering.

**Is a separate webcam better than the one in my laptop?** Usually yes, and mostly because of where it sits rather than the sensor. A camera above the screen looks at your face. A laptop camera looks up at your chin and your ceiling.

**What about a chair?** It matters more than everything on this page put together, and we have [written about that separately](/blog/best-budget-office-chair-uk-2026).

## How we chose these

Our own prices are read live from the retailer as this page loads, so they are current rather than whatever they were when this was written.

The Apple figure is Apple's own published UK price, linked above. It is one of the few premium numbers we quote that does not need re-checking constantly, because Apple does not discount its own hardware and third-party sellers rarely move far from it.

We have not tested these against each other. The ordering above is not a performance ranking and does not pretend to be. It is an argument about sequence, based on which changes people notice, and you are free to disagree with it. What is not in dispute is what each item costs, what it physically does, and the specification gap between a 4K panel and a 5K one.

Where a discount appears it is one the tracker recorded against a price the product genuinely sold at, rather than a permanent sale price that was never anything else.
