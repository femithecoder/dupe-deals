La Mer's iconic Crème de la Mer costs £175 for a 60ml pot. It's a cult product, but the truth is the skincare industry has a well-kept secret — the active ingredients that make it work aren't exclusive to La Mer.

We tested five affordable alternatives available right now in the UK, and here's our honest verdict.

## 1. CeraVe Moisturising Cream — £11.99 at Boots

CeraVe is the number one dermatologist-recommended moisturiser in the US, and it's now widely available in the UK. The key ingredients — ceramides, hyaluronic acid, and niacinamide — are scientifically proven to restore the skin barrier, which is exactly what La Mer claims to do with its "Miracle Broth."

**Verdict:** Best overall dupe. Save over £160 per pot.

## 2. The Ordinary Natural Moisturising Factors — £5.90 at ASOS

No frills, just effective. This contains a blend of amino acids, fatty acids, and hyaluronic acid that mimics the skin's natural moisturising factors. Lightweight and absorbs quickly.

**Verdict:** Best for oily skin types.

## 3. Neutrogena Hydro Boost Water Gel — £14.99 at Superdrug

Hyaluronic acid-based gel moisturiser that delivers intense hydration without heaviness. A favourite among skincare enthusiasts who've tried both.

**Verdict:** Best for combination skin.

## 4. Olay Regenerist Micro-Sculpting Cream — £19.99 at Boots

Contains niacinamide and amino peptides. In blind tests, Olay Regenerist regularly outperforms products ten times its price, including La Mer.

**Verdict:** Best anti-ageing alternative.

## 5. Simple Kind to Skin Moisturiser — £4.49 at Superdrug

No perfume, no harsh chemicals, no unnecessary ingredients. Exactly what sensitive skin needs — and nothing it doesn't.

**Verdict:** Best for sensitive skin.

The bottom line? You don't need to spend £175 on a moisturiser. All five of these are available from trusted UK retailers and deliver results comparable to La Mer at a fraction of the cost.
